I · The idea

It started as a shoe made by hand.

Jacob and Usairam built the first AIR 6770 from cardboard, paint and glue. It had a yellow top crossed with black stripes, aubergine sides, a gold toe and a crimson base. The materials were simple. The idea was not.

Lateral profile · 1 : 1

II · The silhouette

The same line, shaped around a foot.

The long, low profile and the rounded toe of the original stay. Around them, the proportions are corrected: a lower collar at the ankle, a toe box with room to move, and a heel that stands upright on the sole.

Toe box · three-quarter front

III · The signature

Five colours, kept where they were.

Every block of colour in the original survives. Electric-yellow leather over the instep, aubergine suede on the sides, three black patent stripes across the vamp, a metallic-gold toe cap and a crimson rand around the base.

  • Electric yellow
  • Aubergine
  • Patent black
  • Heritage gold
  • Crimson

IV · The foundation

Built up from the ground.

A sculpted ivory cupsole holds amber cushioning chambers you can see at the heel. Above it, a stitched crimson rand. Below it, an espresso rubber outsole with the HZ monogram moulded into the heel.

      Jacob & Usairam

      Two minds. One original.

      The AIR 6770 began as a shoe Jacob and Usairam made by hand. They chose its colours, its stripes and its long, low shape. The HASSAN ZHANG edition keeps every one of those choices and changes only how the shoe is made.

      The yellow, the aubergine, the black stripes, the gold toe and the crimson base all come from their original. Nothing about the colour blocking was added later.
